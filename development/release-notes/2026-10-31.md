# Release Notes: 31 October 2026

## 2. One Robot After Another on One Page

The ROBOT page now moves from one robot to another without being opened again. Before, choosing the dog after the fish left the dog undrawn, both in its side view and in the world, and the camera beneath the ice: the page drew the fish's last pose with the dog's legs, and its drawing stopped. Now choosing a robot clears the poses of the one before, and the robot's body in the world is built anew for each robot, the dog after the rabbit included.

## 1. Robots in the World, the Ground They Learn, and Every Field at a Point

In this update Cosmopoiesis carries three robots of the robotics domain into its world, each on the ground or in the water of its place, and shows every field of the world at a point ([decision 0011](../decisions/0011-a-rabbit-on-the-moon.md), [decision 0012](../decisions/0012-the-ground-a-robot-learns-and-the-fields-at-a-point.md); the infrastructure's ADR 0058 and ADR 0059).

- **The ROBOT page.** Choose a rabbit, a fish or a dog, its site or the point chosen, what to ask of it and the method that moves it (the action's variational integrator, semi-implicit Euler or Runge–Kutta). The page draws its body from the side, or from above for the fish, with each motor's torque against its peak and the ground's force at each foot, and keeps the account of its energy and each method's cost. In the world it stands at its site at true scale, lit by the Sun.
  - **The rabbit** hops on the Moon's regolith at Reiner Gamma; a hop on the Earth is refused, the ankle too weak.
  - **The fish** swims a metre below the sea's surface in the equatorial Pacific, carried by the current, its wake drawn where its tail pushed the water; on land it is refused.
  - **The dog** trots on the Greenland ice sheet or on Paris's sediment, ice, sediment or rock by CRUST1.0; at sea it is refused.
- **The ground a robot learns.** The world presumes what nothing has measured of a place: the Moon's regolith, the ocean's density and current, the Earth's ground by its class, each a field of provenance `model` with its spread. A robot stands on a realisation of it drawn with the world's seed, and its feet read that truth. The page shows what it was told, what its feet read, what it believes along its line, and, revealed, the truth; and whether its readings fit what it was told.
- **Every field at a point.** POINT lists every field of the world at the point chosen (`field.at`), each component with its unit, frame and provenance, and folds away those that have nothing there, each with its reason. The world now has 29 fields, the ground's and the water's among them.
- **A camera that keeps above the ice.** Close to a robot on the Earth, the camera follows the terrain's refined heights and stays above them.
