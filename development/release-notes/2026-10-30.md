# Release Notes: 30 October 2026

## 1. Contributors, Their Terms, and a Steward

In this update Cosmopoiesis records who made it and on what terms, as The Science Infrastructures becomes a project of Serendip Commons Society (the infrastructure's [ADR 0057](https://github.com/huangwanhong-maker/The-Science-Infrastructures/blob/main/development/decisions/0057-a-project-of-serendip-commons-society.md)).

- **Contributors keep their copyright.** `CONTRIBUTORS.txt` names the contributors and states the terms: each keeps the copyright in what they contributed; contributions are made under the same MIT License, to everyone, with a sign-off certifying the Developer Certificate of Origin; there is no contributor agreement. AI assistance is recorded as a tool, never as a contributor.
- **A steward, not an owner.** The Society keeps the project's record and presents it. It holds no intellectual property in Cosmopoiesis and cannot relicense it; anyone may use, change, fork and carry on the work, with or without the Society.
- **The licence is unchanged.** MIT, its copyright line now "Wanhong Huang and the contributors listed in CONTRIBUTORS.txt".

## 2. The World beneath the Ground and the Sea; a Precision Console, and Figures That Separate

In this update the console is redrawn as a precision instrument, its figures can be taken out into windows of their own, and ENV looks beneath the observer to the centre of the Earth ([decision 0009](../decisions/0009-a-precision-console-and-figures-that-separate.md), [decision 0010](../decisions/0010-the-world-beneath-the-ground-and-the-sea.md)).

- **A precision console.** Square panes with corner ticks, hairlines, outlined keys lit rose when chosen, square lamps, and no pop-ups. Labels and keys are set in IBM Plex Sans Condensed, readings in Plex Sans with tabular figures, and clocks, addresses and identifiers in Plex Mono.
- **Figures that separate.** A figure (the field's cube, the rover's map, its track, the section beneath the observer, the column to the centre) moves into a window over the world, where it can be moved, enlarged and docked again. A page whose figure is out keeps it current.
- **Beneath the ground and the sea.** ENV has a section, Beneath, read by a new instrument: the seismic column, CRUST1.0 over PREM. It gives the crust's thickness and kind, the Moho, the sediments, the water and the ice, the speed of P waves beneath the Moho, and the time a P wave takes through the crust. When the observer is underground or under the sea, it also names the layer the observer is in.
- **A section through the observer.** The section follows a geodesic, toward any azimuth, up to ±2000 km. It has three views: the land, the sea and its floor; the crust; and the mantle to 800 km at true scale, with the Earth's curvature drawn. Rock is coloured by its density. Fifty years of Global CMT's earthquakes trace the sinking plates beneath it, and on hover every point says what lies there and which model says so. Its trace is drawn on the world.
- **The column to the centre.** The speeds of P and S waves and the density, against depth, to the centre of the Earth. S waves stop at the liquid outer core.
- **The interior as fields.** The density and the speeds of the Earth's interior are three fields of the world, so FIELD's cube can be filled with them too.
- **Better.** INSTR and BUS read the seismic column as its crust, not as the height of its cell. Values that round to zero at the digits shown, such as a height on the ellipsoid or the deflection of normal gravity, read as zero. The magnetic field's components no longer overflow their tiles. Names on the globe no longer show through the console's glass. On the Moon, the section's legend names only what is drawn. At sea, SRTM's 0 m is no longer taken for the sea floor.
