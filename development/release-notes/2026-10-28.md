# Release Notes: 28 October 2026

## 1. One World, Its Addresses and Its Instruments

In this update Cosmopoiesis runs: its first slice is the Solar System as one world, at any instant from 1849 to 2150, or now, read by instruments on a runtime with deadlines (decisions [0002](../decisions/0002-the-spacetime-slice-first.md), [0003](../decisions/0003-one-world-its-addresses-and-a-console.md) and [0004](../decisions/0004-instruments-and-their-acquisition.md)).

- **One world.** Every body of DE440 is drawn in its place and turned by its rotation model, from a crater's floor to 110 au above the ecliptic, with each planet's orbit and the Milky Way. The Earth and the Moon have terrain and imagery that refine as one comes near, every height saying where it came from; the other bodies have their maps, finer close in. Going to another body is a flight.
- **Addresses.** Any point is written in one line, `moon(43.31 S, 11.36 W)`, `earth(35.36 N, 138.73 E)/aer(45°, 30°, 10 km)` or a direction and distance from the barycentre to the Galaxy's centre. It is read with one parse, answered in every frame and coordinate system of the catalogue, and flown to; a mistyped address is marked where it goes wrong. The catalogue's loops measure how far its standards disagree, the holonomies of the frames read as a connection.
- **Instruments.** Twelve kernels of observation carry every kind of instrument, and families of them are mounted on the observer, on a satellite or at any address: the time scales, a Sun sensor, the bodies in the sky, a GNSS receiver, a magnetometer on WMM2025, a barometer and a radiosonde on the 1976 standard atmosphere, a gravimeter. Physical sources run beside them: NTP, the solar wind and the interplanetary field at L1, the K index, the Sun's X-rays and protons, earthquakes, the user's own stations. Outside its coverage an instrument says why it cannot read. Every reading carries its provenance, and the console colours what was observed, modelled and assimilated apart.
- **Acquisition with deadlines.** A mission clock held by the server is read by every page and instrument. Instruments are released at its ticks and on events, admitted by EDF's test, degraded elastically under overload, dispatched by EDF, by fixed priority with aging, or in order, with physical sources held to their share by a server. The readings are the same whatever the schedule. A page shows the jobs on each worker as they ran, and simulates each policy on the instruments declared.
- **A console** in pink and purple, laid out as a flight deck: clocks and annunciator lamps, multifunction pages for the view, time, frames, layers and the system, the environment, the stream of readings, the instruments and the schedule, and an address and clock unit. Nothing pops up.
- **The formalism** gains events and their addresses, frames as a connection with standards as gauge, the kinds of phenomena on three axes, instruments as kernels with provenance and the determinism of readings, and the timing of acquisition. A note maps an encyclopedia of instruments onto the kernels.

The work's suite counts 149 checks, among them its runtime and a session of its acquisition in the engine; the domains' and foundations' checks hold the science beneath.

## 2. The Founding of Cosmopoiesis

In this update Cosmopoiesis is founded as a work of The Science Infrastructures ([decision 0001](../decisions/0001-the-founding-of-cosmopoiesis.md)): a framework for constructing heterogeneous physical worlds, from a lunar patch to the flow of the galaxies, and simulating them with the agents that inhabit them, composed from the infrastructure's foundations and domains.

- **The formalism** says what a world is: a sheaf of fields on a space cut into regions, with laws as actions and balance laws, coupling as a fibre product over interfaces, conservation across an interface exact by construction, shapes as equations, mechanisms as algebraic varieties, observables as invariants, agents that see only what is delivered to them, scales as a chain of embeddings, and spaces that a field theory may generate. Eleven propositions are stated with their proofs and the checks that will hold them.
- **The architecture** places the work in the infrastructure: what it owns (the description of worlds, the runtime, the records, the engine, the page), what it uses from each foundation and domain and what it asks them to gain first, the engine and its protocol, World JSON, the datasets of the Moon, Mars and the Earth to register, the page's six views, plug-ins, and seven slices by which it will be built, each a world checked against a closed form. It also says what the preliminary design got right and what it changes.
- **The guide, the licence and the logs** for working here are in place; nothing runs yet.

The release notes of this day in the infrastructure's own repository record its side when the submodule is attached.
