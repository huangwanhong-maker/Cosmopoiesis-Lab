# 0001. The Founding of Cosmopoiesis

- **Status:** Accepted, provisionally (the branch, the submodule's registration, the planetary-science domain and the plug-in classes are the user's to decide; architecture §12)
- **Date:** 2026-10-28
- **Decided by:** the user asked for a general-purpose computational framework for constructing and simulating heterogeneous physical worlds (galaxies, the ocean, the Solar System, the Moon's environment) and the adaptive embodied agents in them, through spatial reconstruction, coordinate systems, physical fields, constrained dynamics and agents; gave a preliminary design as background only, to be trusted in nothing but its philosophy of algebraic geometry, theoretical physics and field theory, which is to be kept; and asked that the design be critical, its own, and rooted in The Science Infrastructures. Claude read the preliminary design and the infrastructure in full, designed what follows, and wrote this record.

## 1. Context

The infrastructure holds, written and checked, most of what the preliminary design proposed to build: units (physis), time scales and frames (metrology), the ephemerides and the rotation of the bodies (astronomy), the figure of the Earth (geodesy), the ocean beneath a place and the relief of the Earth (the data library), cell complexes with their invariants (morphe), discrete exterior calculus with the Hodge decomposition on any complex (physis), integrators, linear algebra, task graphs and reproducible random streams (calx), the beginnings of inference (tyche), an API with plug-in contracts, a visualization layer, and five applications with C++ engines built by recipe. The preliminary design (fifteen notes and a formalism in its fifth version, dated 2026-10-28) knew none of it, proposed modules of its own for all of it, and stated its mathematics as a tuple of symbols without a result.

The user's philosophy for the work is that of theoretical physics and algebraic geometry: fields on spaces with geometry, laws as actions, symmetry and gauge, shapes as equations, and spaces that a field theory can generate (as the Complex Atlas already shows, ADR 0040).

## 2. Decision

1. **Cosmopoiesis is a work of the infrastructure**, an application with a research arm, composing the domains and the foundations; it owns the description of worlds, the runtime that advances a composed world, the records of runs, its engine, its server and its page, and nothing general and no object of nature (architecture §3). It is a repository of its own, attached as the submodule `applications/cosmopoiesis-lab`, by the graduation rule of ADR 0026 applied at birth at the user's request, as neuroscience was (ADR 0028).
2. **Its formalism** ([docs/formalism.md](../../docs/formalism.md)): a world is a sheaf of fields on a carrier decomposed into regions; frames form a groupoid; fields are sections of quantity-valued bundles and, discretised, cochains on morphe's complexes with physis's exact calculus; laws are actions and balance laws, hybrid where things collide; coupling is a fibre product over interfaces, and conservation across a shared interface is a theorem of the construction; transformations are passive, physical or gauge, and observables are invariants; bodies are semi-algebraic sets, gravity fields expansions in harmonic polynomials, mechanisms real algebraic varieties; agents are hybrid systems with ports, sensors with footprints and latencies, and controllers that see only delivered readings; scales are a chain of embeddings with a precision budget; a carrier may be generated by a field theory. Eleven propositions, each with its check.
3. **Its architecture** ([docs/architecture.md](../../docs/architecture.md)): a header-only C++20 engine on the foundations and domains, built by recipe as the other engines are and checked on calx `verification`; World, Agent and Experiment JSON as real schemas; a runtime of preparation, task-graph schedules, trial and commit, snapshots, checkpoints and manifests with commits; a page on the visualization layer with six views, port 8930, terracotta provisionally; the datasets of the Moon, Mars and the Earth's gravity and bathymetry registered in the data library; plug-ins under the API's contract.
4. **Its growth by slices**, each a world that runs end to end and is checked against a closed form (architecture §9), each naming what the foundations must gain first, which is written there and used here: the thermal box; two bodies and one interface; the lunar patch with a rover; the marine basin with a submersible; ocean and atmosphere; the Solar System and the galaxy flow; adaptation.
5. **What is kept of the preliminary design**: its separations (observation, reconstruction, simulation, display; passive change, physical change, gauge; the controller's causal boundary; verification against validation; independent visual, observational and numerical resolution), its heat-equation fixture, and its philosophy. What is replaced: everything it would have built that the infrastructure holds, its tuple in favour of structure with results, its stage plan in favour of the slices, its vocabulary in favour of the infrastructure's. The design itself is not committed; it is kept beside the repository, ignored by git, as background.

## 3. Alternatives Considered

- **Following the preliminary design's stage plan** (units, static transforms, epochs first). These exist; a work that rebuilt them would be a second infrastructure.
- **A new foundation for simulation** (meshes, solvers, coupling). Meshes are morphe's complexes, solvers calx's, the calculus physis's; the coupling of open systems is general and goes to physis `discretisation` and `dynamics` as it is written; what remains is composition, which is a work's.
- **A domain rather than a work.** A domain holds one science's objects; Cosmopoiesis composes many sciences' objects for a purpose, which is the definition of a work. The objects it needs that no domain holds (the Moon as a world) found a domain, planetary science, rather than live here.
- **Borrowing a simulation framework** (Chrono, preCICE, PETSc) as the core. The infrastructure owns its interfaces and borrows implementations behind them where it serves; none is adopted now, and the first slices need nothing a foundation does not have or cannot gain.

- **In the infrastructure**, to be proposed to the user: a record in its decision log founding the work and attaching the submodule; its CLAUDE.md §§1, 3.1, 5, 7 and 9, its README's §5 and §6 and the applications' index naming Cosmopoiesis and its port; the planetary-science domain brought forward from tier 3.6 when slice 2 begins; the plug-in classes in the API when slice 2 needs them.
